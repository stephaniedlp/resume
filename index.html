about me

A curious mind.

I'm Stephanie — a Full Stack Software Engineer who builds scalable systems by day and finds beauty in small moments at every other hour. My world lives at the intersection of clean code, specialty coffee, and light hitting a lens at the right angle.

As a barista, I've learned that precision, timing, and intuition matter as much in a cup of coffee as they do in a well-designed API. As a photographer, I frame the world with the same intentionality I bring to architecture diagrams — every shot a decision about what to include and what to leave out.
